This button leads to a part of FigDot that is not open yet. Here is what works today, and what is coming.

What works today

  • A public demo collection, Les AOP fromagères du Puy-de-Dôme: three figures, each with its framing, its options, its definitions and its dated, linked sources.
  • For every figure: a stable address for each option, the data (CSV), the image (SVG, PNG), and an embed code you compose module by module.
  • 10 shapes in the catalogue: radar, flow diagram, beeswarm, slope chart, map, scatter plot, timeline, range bars, matrix, bars.
  • The FigDot widget for Grist, which prepares figure drafts in a Grist document.

What is coming

  • Accounts and sign-in.
  • Your design system drafted from your documents: a brand guide, a report or a site, turned into a design system that FigDot checks and you approve. Today a design system is written by hand in FigDot's format.
  • The agent through a connector: describe a figure to your AI agent, which drafts it in FigDot's format.
  • Online review: read, comment on and approve a figure before it is published.
  • More shapes.

No date is promised. To know more, write to contact@plurall.cc.
