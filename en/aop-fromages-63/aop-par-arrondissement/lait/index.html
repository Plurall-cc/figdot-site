PDO cheeses across the five arrondissements

Ripening included, the Saint-Nectaire area reaches all 5 arrondissements of the département; the Fourme de Montbrison area only touches that of Ambert.

  • no commune
  • 1 to 10
  • 11 to 50
  • 51 to 100
  • over 100

Communes per arrondissement: Ambert 58, Clermont-Ferrand 73, Issoire 133, Riom 155, Thiers 44.

Only four plots of Sayat lie within the Cantal area and the Salers area; it is counted as a whole commune, as in the INAO list.

Perimeter:
the 463 communes of Puy-de-Dôme in the 2025 official geographic code; 7 former codes of the INAO list counted in their current commune, which is already listed; Sayat, partly within the Cantal and Salers area, counted whole

PDO cheeses across the five arrondissements

For milk and cheese-making, the Saint-Nectaire production zone fits in 2 arrondissements: 46 communes in that of Issoire, 3 in that of Clermont-Ferrand.

  • 1 to 10
  • 11 to 50

Communes per arrondissement: Ambert 58, Clermont-Ferrand 73, Issoire 133, Riom 155, Thiers 44.

Only four plots of Sayat lie within the Cantal area and the Salers area; it is counted as a whole commune, as in the INAO list.

Unit:
communes of each arrondissement
Option:
milk production and cheese-making; only Saint-Nectaire changes, its production zone covers 49 communes of the département instead of 463; the other five appellations have a single area
Perimeter:
the 463 communes of Puy-de-Dôme in the 2025 official geographic code; 7 former codes of the INAO list counted in their current commune, which is already listed; Sayat, partly within the Cantal and Salers area, counted whole
