How many Puy-de-Dôme communes each PDO cheese covers

For milk and cheese-making, the Fourme d'Ambert area is the largest, 292 communes; the Saint-Nectaire production zone has 49.

  • Whole area, ripening includedThe communes of each appellation's geographic area, as given by the INAO list; it includes ripening.
  • Milk production and cheese-makingThe communes where the milk may be produced and the cheese made. Only the Saint-Nectaire specification separates this zone from the geographic area; for the other five appellations, it is the same area.
  • communes of the geographic area
  • milk production and cheese-making zone

Only four plots of Sayat lie within the Cantal area and the Salers area; it is counted as a whole commune, as in the INAO list.
